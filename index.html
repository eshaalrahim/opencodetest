Bunny Café

Cozy resting place in between work sessions

Welcome to the Bunny Café, a virtual burrow where you can pause, take a deep breath, and imagine ordering a warm drink from the menu below. Picture yourself interacting with our friendly bunny workers who are ready to serve you – all without waiting in line or fighting for a seat. It’s your cozy escape anytime.

Why Bunnies?

Bunnies are one of my favorite animals! I discovered my love for them a few years back when I took care of a family's pet rabbit while they were out of town. His name was Baby, and he was very fat, fluffy, and lazy, so he just laid there looking cute all day. I've never had any pets of my own, but I would love to adopt a bunny one day! :)

Why a Café?

As a college student, cafes are a GODSEND. I only wish that other college students didn't also love them because it can be so hard to find seating in them in Boston. I like the idea of having some kind of virtual cafe with the same cozy yet productive vibe of actual cafes but with unlimited seating, though I'm not sure how getting visitors caffeinated would work :P For now, the AI-generated menu above will have to make do. Enjoy your fictional sipping!
